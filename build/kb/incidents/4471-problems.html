parser: the shared parser reads every date month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so day and month swap: a booking for 7 April goes out for 4 July. This covers reports from the app, from email, and from tenants on booking-v2, whose form hint is DD/MM/YYYY.

hint: booking-v3 prints the wrong hint under its date field

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and reads what people type the tenant’s way, day first. People who follow the hint get their dates swapped. Fixing the parser does not fix this: the form has to print the hint its tenant reads.

one-day-move: dates move by one day, not by a swap

These reports describe a date one day off: an invite on Tuesday 12 May with the calendar on Monday 11 May, the same meeting on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, June bookings one day off, an invite that arrived before its booking. No day and month swap explains them. A time zone or day-boundary fault is the likely cause; the export has no event times to prove it. The desk already listed these reports in desk-notes.json; causes.ts still files them under parser by tenant.

How #4471 got here

  • Triage merges reports when their subject lines match (decision 0001). Subject says nothing about cause, so all three problems landed in one ticket.
  • Intake tags come from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. They disagree with the cause in both directions.
  • The ticket export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Each report counts once, by id.
